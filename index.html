I. The Lane & the Window

A quiet residential lane at dusk. Amber evening light angles through overhead wires and dusty roadside trees. Saurabh pedals a bicycle alone in casual non-black clothes toward an evening gathering, his rhythm steady and unhurried.

To his right, an imposing residence rises behind an old stone boundary wall topped with an iron railing. At an open second-story window, three South Asian women appear in traditional red and black clothing—each possessing an unmistakable, distinct face. One woman raises a hand in a quiet, deliberate beckoning motion toward the entrance; the other two watch him in silence. A sudden wave of déjà vu takes hold: he feels with certainty that he has encountered these women before, and that he has accidentally worn the wrong clothes. He brakes, turns his bicycle around, and rides back home.

II. The Black Armor

Inside his modest home, he leaves his bicycle in the hallway and walks directly to his bedroom. He lays out black trousers, a plain black crewneck T-shirt, a structured black utility jacket, and heavy black leather boots.

He strips off his casual attire and dresses methodically into the black ensemble. Checking his reflection in the mirror, his face remains the same, but his presence is hardened into uniform black. Fastened into this armor, he wheels his bicycle back out onto the street.

III. The Shortcut & the Loop

Saurabh rides back along the same road, passing beneath the stone wall and the high window. Further down the lane, he notices an open, unremarkable entrance in a concrete house beside the road. Assuming it will cut through to the parallel street, he parks his bicycle near the doorway and steps inside on foot.

The threshold immediately collapses into a spatial impossibility. Symmetrical, unpainted concrete hallways stretch into dim shadow. He turns through what should be an exterior exit, only to enter another identical corridor. He searches left and right; a metal door remains locked; corridors bend back onto themselves. Reaching a junction, he identifies a recognizable crack in the wall—he has looped in a circle. The building is an architectural maze with no exterior exit.

IV. The Shutter & Baribari

Tired, breathing heavily against the concrete wall, he notices a warm glow seeping from beneath a small partition. He approaches and knocks firmly on the corrugated metal shutter.

The shutter rises. Standing behind the counter is Baribari—a composed young woman with expressive dark eyes, her hair neatly bound, wearing a deep maroon traditional salwar-kameez embroidered with subtle threadwork. She studies him cautiously:

“Who are you? How did you get in here?”
“I took a shortcut through the house,” Saurabh answers. “And I’m lost.”

She unlocks the secondary shutter and releases him into the exit hall. Over the counter, an instant rapport forms. He asks her name; she replies: “Baribari.” When he asks for her surname so he can locate her later, she tells him. He invites her to join him at the party down the road. Baribari smiles, drapes a light shawl over her shoulders, and steps out into the night beside him.

V. The Gathering & the Night Walk

They arrive together at a warm, modest home party. Saurabh introduces Baribari to his friends simply by her first name. Seeing them together, friends assume without question that they are a couple and remark that they look right together. Neither Saurabh nor Baribari corrects them; they sit beside a window, sharing an unhurried, comfortable intimacy.

When the gathering winds down, they leave together. Saurabh walks Baribari home through the deserted, nocturnal streets.

VI. The Narrow Gate

Without sound or warning, Baribari’s body goes slack. She slips into sudden unconsciousness and begins to fall. Saurabh lunges and catches her before she hits the ground. He calls her name urgently, but receives no response. He lifts her onto his back, carrying her through the darkened road until they arrive at her home: an abrasive stone boundary wall interrupted by an exceedingly narrow iron gate.

With Baribari unconscious across his shoulders, Saurabh reaches up to grip the jagged masonry of the wall, carefully shifting his footing sideways through the restricted gap, shielding her waist from the rough stone. As her weight slips backward, he pivots and catches her securely in his arms, carrying her inside to her alarmed, grateful family. Standing back, watching her rest, an overwhelming certainty fills him: he never wants to lose her; he wants to share his life with her.

VII. The Flare & the Vanishing Name

A blinding, silent white flare blooms across the screen, obliterating the night.

The flare cools into sterile morning light across the ceiling of Saurabh’s actual bedroom. Saurabh wakes with a sharp intake of breath, his hands clenched into the bedsheets. The entire sequence—the three women at the window, the black armor, the repeating corridors, the party, the narrow gate—was an unbroken dream.

He sits up in the quiet morning light, heart pounding, desperately searching his memory for her surname so he can find her in the waking world.

He remembers the face. He remembers the voice. He remembers the name “Baribari.”
Her surname is gone. Fade slowly to black.

The Format

One panel. Eight seconds. Full attention.

AxisChronicle films are assembled frame by frame, with every composition held long enough to be read.

01

Eight-second panels

Each panel is a composed image held for exactly eight seconds, so the eye has time to settle and notice.

02

Voice and room tone

Measured voiceover and spatial room tone carry the story. Silence is part of the structure.

03

Continuity by design

Characters, wardrobe and locations are specified frame to frame, so nothing breaks the spell.
