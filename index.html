The film · Episode 01

A name remains after the dream.

A vivid dream leaves Samar with one name: Baribari. By morning, the memory feels close, yet impossible to hold.

Was it only a dream, or a fragment of something real? The answer slips between what he remembers and what the morning cannot confirm.

“Some dreams leave more questions than memories.”

BARIBARI is a psychological mystery about the fragile border between dreaming and waking.
